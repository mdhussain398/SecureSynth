# Speaker intent

About 40 seconds per slide, except the demo (2 minutes). These lines are also written into the deck's speaker notes.

1. **Title.** Say the claim and stop: "A correct password and OTP tell you someone has the credentials. They don't tell you the customer is there. That's what we built SecureSynth to check."
2. **The gap.** Walk through the three attacks in one breath each. Land on the number: password plus OTP stopped none of 367 takeovers in our test.
3. **Static rules.** "The usual fix is rules." Point at 98.6%: nearly every traveller gets challenged. That's the friction banks live with.
4. **Keep asking.** Trace the loop with your hand: login, payee, transfer, each one scored, the backend enforcing the answer. Mention the trusted device and number matching.
5. **Normal is personal.** The 2 a.m. example. One shared model works because every feature is already personal. Point at the panel that says "not a probability".
6. **Learning safely.** "If we learned from everything, an attacker could teach us they belong." Explain candidate versus trusted in two sentences.
7. **The result.** Read the two pairs of numbers slowly. Say "about a fifth of the friction". Mention the protocol was written before we saw results.
8. **Where it doesn't win.** Don't rush this slide. Say each card plainly. Judges trust the team that shows its weak spot.
9. **After the block.** "Most demos stop at BLOCK. That's where a real analyst starts." Name the five stages.
10. **Evidence.** Explain the hash chain in one sentence. Say "tamper-evident, not immutable" and why.
11. **Leads, not proof.** Point at one strong link and its explanation. Stress that nothing auto-acts.
12. **Demo.** Run the takeover in Judge mode. Narrate: right password, right OTP, wrong person. If it fails, switch to the fallback stills without apologising.
13. **Built to be checked.** Four numbers, then move on.
14. **Next.** Be specific about what production needs. Invite the question about real data.
15. **Close.** Repeat the one line. Thank them. Open for questions.
