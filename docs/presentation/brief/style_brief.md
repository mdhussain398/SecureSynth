# Style brief: Apple keynote language

- One idea per slide. The title states the claim as a sentence; the slide proves it.
- Large type, lots of space. Titles 36 to 40 pt bold, body 16 to 20 pt, captions 12 pt. Nothing smaller than 12 pt.
- Typeface: Helvetica Neue (on every Mac; closest to SF Pro, which Office can't embed). Windows falls back to Arial.
- Colour: near-black `0B0F17` and white `FFFFFF` dominate. One accent, SecureSynth electric blue `0A84FF` (Apple system blue). Status colours only for meaning: green `30D158` allow, orange `FF9F0A` step-up, red `FF453A` block.
- Dark slides for the title, the graph, the demo and the close; light slides for argument and evidence.
- Screenshots sit in rounded frames (radius about 0.18 in) with a soft shadow, never cropped awkwardly or stretched.
- No decorative bars, no underlines under titles, no emoji, no clip art. Icons are Lucide line icons in tinted rounded squares, the same motif the app uses.
- Numbers get their own size: 72 to 96 pt for headline stats, with a short label beneath.
- Left-align body text. Centre only the title and closing slides.
